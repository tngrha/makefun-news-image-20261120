# Winner study — news-image-20261120 dense rebuild (2026-11-20, build session)

## What the winner does that our old renderer does not

Winner (news-image-01, gentle-meteor-30, 858903, $50) vs our 20261117 round-2 entry
(place 4,366/7,904, 486,059; paid cutoff 627,214; winner 849,510):

1. **Density**: 35 stories vs our 12 (masthead + 1 hero + 8 tiles + 3 briefs).
   The judge criteria put "selection and breadth" second — 12 stories cannot compete.
2. **Illustrated panels**: 11 panels with original drawings. Our tiles were text-only.
3. **World map with numbered pins 1–11 + legend**: makes breadth visible at a glance
   and ties panels to geography. We had no map at all.
4. **24 one-line briefs** ("ALSO ON FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER" 12–35, two columns): the tail
   stories that push the count to 35. We had 3 briefs.
5. **Masthead with the count**: "35 STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ONE DAY" + serif date —
   the breadth claim is visible before any story is read.
6. **Sources footer**: signals faithfulness (criterion 4).
7. **Editorial white front page**: high contrast, reads as a newspaper at thumbnail size.

Our v3 reference (image-v2/news-v3.jpg) copied the structure but: no panel
illustrations, oversized black silhouette map, rendered via Chrome (not the sandbox
renderer), and never submitted (competitions closed).

## What I will do BEYOND the winner (level up)

1. **Same 35-story density** (1 hero + 11 illustrated panels + 23 numbered briefs),
   every story verified live from 20 Nov 2026 reporting, each with one concrete
   number/fact. Disputed claims attributed ("Poland says", "Pakistan says").
2. **Color-coded region badges** on every panel (EUROPE blue, ASIA red, MIDDLE EAST
   orange, AFRICA green, NORTH AMERICA purple, SOUTH AMERICA teal, OCEANIA brown,
   GLOBAL navy) — the winner used plain text labels; color makes breadth scannable
   at thumbnail size and matches pin colors on the map.
3. **Numbered pins colored to match panel badges** + a two-line legend under the map.
4. **A "DAY IN NUMBERS" hook strip** under the masthead (9–6 ICJ vote · 110 missiles
   on Kyiv · $81.3B IPO · 100% global debt · $2.3B GTA 6) — a level-up the winner
   lacks; it front-loads the concrete-facts criterion.
5. **Bigger hero headline type** (scale 4) so the lead story reads at ~300px, and
   panel headlines at scale 2 with pseudo-bold labels.
6. **Flat geometric pictograms** drawn in code for all 12 illustrated stories
   (skyline, missile arc, tower, courthouse, chart arrow, globe, jet, radar, virus,
   flags, spacecraft) — original drawings like the winner, generated by the
   deterministic renderer.
7. **Lighter dotted-grid world map** (simplified continent polygons, equirectangular)
   instead of a heavy black silhouette.
